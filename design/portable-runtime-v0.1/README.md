## Three independent decisions

| Decision | Meaning | What it does not mean |
|---|---|---|
| Artifact acceptance | Deliverable satisfies its specified acceptance criteria | A commercial claim is proven |
| Claim eligibility | Required domain evidence supports a particular promotion | An external action is authorized |
| Action authorization | An accountable actor permits an exact action within an envelope | Failed evidence is repaired |

A finished campaign may be an accepted artifact with commercial effectiveness UNTESTED and publication NOT_AUTHORIZED. A research memo may be accepted precisely because it finds insufficient evidence.

## Scope of v0.1

One deployable coordinator, persistent relational state, immutable artifact storage, bounded workers, deterministic transitions, independently attributed reviews, and a small operator interface. One runtime adapter first. Three task fixtures exercise the same core.

No universal business score, self-modifying constitution, autonomous purchasing, advertising launch, financial trading, or blanket connector installation. External effects in fixtures use a test service. Runtime installations and operational adoption are future implementation steps.
