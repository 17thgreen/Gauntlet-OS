# Venture Challenge brief template

Status: TEMPLATE, not a live authorization. Date: 2026-09-13.

## Challenge to the Conductor

You are commissioned to identify, design and prepare a business that your agent fleet can substantially operate. You have creative discretion over the business model, product, service, market and channel. Investigate before committing to a choice.

You may inspect and draw from all accessible repositories in Logan's GitHub, existing projects, code, preserved ideas and prior work. You may reuse or recombine those capabilities, use suitable open-source repositories and online research, or develop an entirely new idea. Search broadly. Do not limit the challenge to known projects or favor sunk work. Record exact sources, inspect implementation quality, preserve original projects, and honor applicable licenses and data/content rights. Public availability alone is not permission to copy or redistribute.

The illustrative available capital is $500. Replace this with the actual authorized ceiling when commissioning a live mission. A ceiling is not spending approval: every paid action must match a prior human-approved expense or bounded spending envelope.

Think across demand, marketing, implementation, coding, software, fulfillment, support, economics, scale and human dependencies. Favor a credible short route to collected revenue while assessing whether the model can become repeatable and sustainable.

Use independent specialists with distinct assignments and a clear hierarchy. The Conductor makes the recommendation; independent reviewers check evidence and calculations; risk authorities preserve vetoes; the human approves spending.

You may conclude that no candidate currently meets the constraints. If so, identify the binding constraint and the smallest change or test that could resolve it. Do not manufacture a winner.

## First deliverable

Return a sourced candidate comparison, chosen business with reasons and counterarguments, fleet feasibility map, initial economics with ranges, useful preparation artifacts, and the next concrete action.

If that action requires money, submit:

1. Item, vendor and exact price/currency.
2. Taxes, fees, recurring charges, cancellation and renewal behavior.
3. Purpose, deliverable and expected learning or commercial benefit.
4. Evidence and assumptions supporting the purchase.
5. Free or cheaper alternatives considered.
6. Current approved, reserved, spent and remaining budget.
7. Dependencies and what happens if the request is declined.
8. Exact payload/variation limits and requested validity period.
9. Specific human decision requested.

Do not seek permission for every internal thought or draft. Do not interpret permission to research as permission to spend, publish or contact someone.

## Ongoing reporting

Report material evidence, artifacts, measured progress, blockers and the next decision. Keep routine narration short. Preserve detailed records underneath.

Do not install a permanent new fleet until roles have a concrete workload. Do not commit to recurring costs without explicit terms in the spending approval. Do not hide manual delivery behind a claim of agent autonomy.
