## Established research constraints

- Preserve the registered chronological **60% research / 20% validation / 20% historical sealed holdout** split and exact recorded boundaries.
- Preserve the provisional **7 bps round-trip cost stack [A]** and registered **1×/2×/3×** stresses until prospectively replaced by verified venue-specific economics.

- Require a true future forward window containing data that did not exist when the strategy was frozen, followed by the remaining promotion gates.
- Preserve holdout, minimum-sample, perturbation, cost, failure and authority rules from the authoritative live records. Unrecovered numeric settings are [U], not invented here.
