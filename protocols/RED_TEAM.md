# Red Team protocol

Review apparent survivors before promotion, then reopen review when evidence or operating conditions materially change.

## Attack plan

- Check whether inputs were knowable at decision time and whether labels or outcomes leaked.
- Audit candidate families, repeated trials, selection and reported exclusions.
- Test nearby parameters, alternate periods, subpopulations/instruments and failure regimes.
- Apply placebo, reversed/time-shifted signals where meaningful, and component ablations.
- Remove exceptional outcomes to assess concentration.
- Stress costs, delay, liquidity, capacity and execution feasibility where relevant.
- Require independent-context replication and future observations according to the domain pack.
- Distinguish a predictive result from proof of its causal explanation.

Numerical stress levels and pass thresholds must come from the registered experiment. Suggested attacks are not silently inserted as retrospective acceptance criteria.

## Findings and resolution

Create a finding with ID, candidate/version, precise objection, evidence label, reproducer or proposed falsification test, materiality, affected gate, owner and closure requirements.

An unresolved material validity finding holds promotion. The Conductor routes remediation; an independent Examiner executes tests; the Prosecutor records whether the objection is resolved. Data, execution and risk vetoes remain with their own authorities.

Archive dissent, unsuccessful attacks and unresolved unknowns. Do not demand agreement. Do not confuse “no defect found in this review” with “the claim is universally true.”
