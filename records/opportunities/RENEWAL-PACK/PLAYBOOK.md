# Renewal Pack — operational playbook

**Status:** PREPARED opening argument. Not a fleet. Authority NONE.  
**Date:** 2026-09-18  
**Tape:** Georgia entity + trade-license calendar vs the shop that is still bidding.  
**Doctrine:** Sell the pack, not a compliance platform (Billow / Yaman).

This playbook is the strongest first approach we can defend today. A future fleet may replace the customer, price, or object with a better argument. It may not invent revenue.

---

## 1. Opportunity and recommendation

**Who pays.** A Georgia trade that must stay in good standing to bid work: HVAC, plumbing, electrical, residential / light-commercial GC, and the LLC those people already formed. Secondary payer: the GC or agency that will not let an expired shop on a job.

**What they receive.** A **job-ready Renewal Pack**, not a login:

- Current Georgia Corporations Division status print (control number, registered agent, good-standing / dissolved).
- Filed annual registration receipt when they authorize us to file (state fee passed through).
- License-board status for the qualifying individual (Residential Basic / Light Commercial / commercial GC / trade board if in-scope).
- Optional: current COI facesheet if their broker emails it — we do not bind insurance.
- One page: CLEAR / NOT CLEAR, with the next date on the calendar.

**Why this approach first.** The public clock is already printed.

- Georgia annual registration is due **April 1** every year ($60 most profit entities / LLCs; $40 nonprofit; **$25** late penalty; then administrative dissolution). Filing window opens January 1. Advance filing up to three years is allowed. [V] SOS how-to.
- Residential and commercial general contractor licenses renew **by June 30 of even-numbered years**. 2026 already passed; next on-time window is **2028**. Late windows and reinstatement are expensive and can require exam. Residential CE is real (3h/yr Basic, 6h/yr Light Commercial) and as of 2026 must be logged in CE Broker. [V] SOS board pages.
- Plumbers and other Construction Industry Licensing Board trades have their own even-year dates (plumbers often November 30). Do not smash every trade into June 30. [I] secondary guides; confirm per board before a letter.

Harbor Compliance sells the same SOS filing nationally at **$199 per state plus the $60**. LegalZoom lists **$99 + state fee**. [V] their price pages, 2026. We will not win “we file all fifty states.” We win **Georgia trade + license + pack a GC can staple**, delivered like mail, not like SaaS.

**Recommendation (provisional).** First product is **SOS annual registration + status pack** for Georgia LLCs in one trade, sold in the January–March 2027 window. License-board pack is the 2028 add-on, sold from the same list. Do not lead with software. Do not lead with “AI compliance.”

## 3. Competing models

**A. National filer (Harbor, LegalZoom, registered-agent mills).**  
Wins on multi-state and “set it and forget it.” Loses on license boards, CE Broker, COI, and a pack a superintendent will accept. Price floor ~$99–$199 + $60. We do not undercut them on fifty-state RA.

**B. SaaS reminder / dashboard.**  
Calendar app, $9/month. Billow already ran the experiment: CFOs did not buy the tool. Salon version that worked was one sentence: “it texts before they forget.” A dashboard is the wrong object.

**C. Bookkeeper / CPA who files April 1 as a courtesy.**  
The real incumbent. If the shop already has one who files, we lose SOS-only. We still win if they do **not** track the qualifying individual’s license or the COI date — that is the pack.

**D. Recommended: Georgia Renewal Pack (outcome).**  
One trade, one state, one calendar, physical or PDF pack, authorization to file SOS when paid. License and COI layered on the same relationship. Provisional winner because first dollar does not require a product and the clock is public.

**E. Rejected: file without consent, scrape GOALS logins, “AI lawyer.”** Hard gates.

## 5. Economics

**Unit (SOS pack, assumed).**

- Revenue: $209 ($149 + $60) of which $60 leaves the same week.
- Contribution after state: $149.
- Variable: postage ~$2, print ~$1, SOS session ~15–25 human minutes first year, payment processing ~3%.
- If 20 minutes at a fully loaded $60/hr: $20 labor. Contribution ~$120 before acquisition.

**Acquisition.** Letter + list pull. 30 letters ~$40 + 2 hours research. If 2 of 30 pay, CAC ~$20–$40 cash + time — acceptable. If 0 of 30, tape is sick.

**Working capital.** State fee should be charged on their card at authorization, not floated. Do not carry $60 × N.

**Downside.** Admin-dissolution advice that was wrong; filing the wrong registered-agent address; UPL letter; Harbor price war; April crunch (every filer in the state hits eCorp the same week).

**Human cap.** Year-one target: ≤ 25 minutes per paying pack, ≤ 4 hours/week in March. If the operation needs a full-time April, we failed automation or we took too many files. Measure minutes. Do not claim 95%.

**Repeat.** Annual SOS is the retainer. 2028 license is the upsell. Do not model $25M.

## 6. Operating workflow

1. **Find.** SOS entity search + license-board roster for one trade. Join on person name / company name. Store control number, RA address, license number, next date. Fail closed on ambiguous joins.
2. **Offer.** One-page letter: dates, price, “we file SOS when you sign; you still click GOALS for the license.” Reply by mail, text, or a Typeform that is not “the product.”
3. **Authorize.** Written authority: legal name, control number, registered agent as they want it, card for $149+$60, statement they authorize us to submit the 2027 annual registration. No file without this.
4. **Deliver.** Submit on eCorp / One Click. Save confirmation PDF. Build pack: status print + receipt + license snapshot + CLEAR / NOT CLEAR. Email + one paper copy.
5. **Collect.** Service fee captured at authorization. State fee same day. No net-30.
6. **Support.** If SOS rejects (bad RA address, dissolved already), stop and tell them. We do not invent a new entity.
7. **Reorder.** December letter: “2028 window opens January 1. Reply YES.” Prepaid three-year SOS filing is an upsell ($60 × 3 + one service fee), only if they want it.

Exceptions (human): dissolved entities, name mismatches, CE-deficient licenses, anyone asking “should I sue / should I form a new LLC.”

## 8. First experiment

**Object.** Will two Georgia trade LLCs pay $149+$60 for a 2027 SOS file + status pack from a letter, without a product?

**Setup (cheapest).**

- N = 30 entities, one trade, one region, good standing, not already prepaid multi-year if we can see it.
- One letter, one follow-up. Budget request: postage + list time only. No ads.
- Baseline: 0 paid. Incumbent is “they remember in March” or Harbor.
- Clock: mail date, reply date, paid date, filed date, pack-sent date, minutes.

**Green.** ≥ 2 paid authorizations and ≥ 1 completed file with pack that matches live SOS.  
**Watch.** 1 paid. Improve letter, do not build software.  
**Kill.** 0 paid after follow-up, or they only want a free reminder, or UPL/SOS blocks third-party file.

**Stop.** Do not file without authorization. Do not expand to 500 letters to rescue a zero. Do not add GeeLark, Grok-on-eCorp, or a dashboard mid-test.

Window: list work can start now; **do not collect 2027 fees before January 2027** unless they want a prepaid multi-year on the current SOS rules. Letters in January.
