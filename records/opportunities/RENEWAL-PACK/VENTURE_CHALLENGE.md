# Venture Challenge — Renewal Pack

**Status:** Commission text only. Does not start a fleet or grant spend.  
**Attached playbook:** [PLAYBOOK.md](PLAYBOOK.md)

## Commission

Design the strongest Georgia Renewal Pack business you can defend, then show the first complete path from a public SOS/license row to a collected dollar and a pack that matches live state status.

You may change customer, price, channel, or object if you beat the playbook on first-dollar speed, contribution after human minutes, or reliability. You may conclude the idea should stay parked. You may not invent customers, filings, or revenue.

## Attack these claims specifically

1. $149 + $60 is payable by a trade LLC that can already One Click file for $60.
2. Harbor / LegalZoom / the bookkeeper are not already the whole market.
3. A third party can file annual registration with written authority without becoming their lawyer or registered agent.
4. License-board dates can be joined to SOS entities at usable precision (name collision rate).
5. April crunch and eCorp failures are operationally survivable at 30 files.

## Standing objectives

Fastest credible collected dollar. Strong net after human time. Simple delivery. Automation measured in minutes. Repeat April / 2028 license without a full-time calendar clerk.

## Anti-theater

Numbers labeled. Capabilities labeled working / blocked / unbuilt. One workflow: list row → auth → payment → file or honest reject → pack. Empty book stays empty. Minutes recorded. Eligibility (authority, no UPL, no unsupervised GOALS) is a gate.

## Out of scope until a DR

Live SOS submits, ads, other states, insurance binding, practicing law, multi-account anything, Grok driving eCorp.
