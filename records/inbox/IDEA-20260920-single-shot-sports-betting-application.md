## Experiment interpretation

“Single-shot” means one comprehensive initial instruction, allowing the recipient to perform multiple internal research, coding and testing steps. Any human repair prompt should be recorded as a subsequent round, not silently counted as original single-shot success.

Functional build success and demonstrated betting profitability are separate outcomes. Future performance requires elapsed games and independent evaluation; a build-session backtest cannot substitute for that.
