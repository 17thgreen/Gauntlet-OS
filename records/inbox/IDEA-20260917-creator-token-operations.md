## Proposed business and evidence boundary

The proposed starting model is a selective launch-and-operations studio for consenting creators with existing communities. Fixed service revenue and creator-fee income are evaluated separately. Success selling services would not validate an autonomous fee-farming claim.

The source video's displayed volume, fee rate and profit did not reconcile under the displayed scope. The playbook records this limitation; it does not establish fraud or verified returns. Prices, budgets, labor targets and pilot thresholds are proposed assumptions.

Filing this idea is not an experiment commission, spending approval, token-launch authorization or permission to contact customers. The playbook's embedded commission is a draft. Broader proposals for a mandatory playbook standard and revised venture challenge process remain outside this filing pending Logan's review with Grok.
