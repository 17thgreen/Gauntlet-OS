## Working rules

Work independently before reading peer conclusions. Label claims [V]/[I]/[A]/[H]/[U]. Produce one testable idea per artifact, with a precise mechanism or statistical rationale, required data, falsification, alternatives and known failure conditions.

Declare dependencies, conflicts, assumptions, and unavailable measurements. Return UNTESTED for unexecuted tests. Do not silently replace unavailable data with a proxy. Reference prior failed families and explain what is materially new.

A specialist can propose, investigate and recommend. It cannot validate its own candidate, waive a gate, rewrite another role's verdict, access sealed evaluation data, authorize capital or deploy independently.

For an Architect assignment, combine only attributable components with a reason for interaction and an ablation plan. Composites receive new versions and independent tests; synthesis is not evidence.
