# Authority model

Authority follows a narrow mandate, not model intelligence or majority support.

| Function | May do | May block | Must not do |
|---|---|---|---|
| Conductor | Commission, route, prioritize, manage freezes and record eligible transitions | Out-of-scope, duplicate, or unfunded research | Waive failed gates, invent evidence, authorize capital alone, staff Discovery from an Experiment queue to protect current candidates |
| Challenger / Discovery | Attack a working machine, fork the object, write a Challenge packet and Experiment Commission | Flag a commission that copies the working machine | Certify its own opportunity, score tests, grant operating authority |
| Independent specialist | Form atomic hypotheses and propose tests | Flag missing inputs within mandate | Self-validate, deploy, rewrite another actor's finding |
| Architect / synthesizer | Combine attributable components; propose ablation tests | Reject unjustified complexity | Treat synthesis or voting as validation |
| Data governor (Clock) | Audit provenance, timing, suitability, access and quality | Quarantine or reject invalid data and dependent runs | Approve unverifiable temporal semantics |
| Examiner | Execute reproducible tests; issue scoped evidence verdicts | Fail or block measurement gates | Tune a submitted claim to make it pass; grant authority |
| Prosecutor / Red Team | Challenge winners, demand falsification and log dissent | Hold promotion on unresolved material validity findings | Negotiate away evidence requirements |
| Mechanic / execution governor | Build and repair the instrument; assess operational controls | Veto impossible or unverified execution | Invent fills or equate a packet with fulfillment |
| Rights / consent governor | Encode approved permission rules | Block delivery that lacks bound evidence | Issue legal opinions; counsel remains human |
| Risk/capital governor (Treasurer) | Set approved exposure envelope; authorize, reduce or halt spend | Hard veto within its risk mandate | Certify a failed hypothesis as valid; treat customer revenue as our budget |
| Operator | Fulfill under granted authority; run exceptions; report workload | Flag deterioration and request restriction | Expand object, customer, or risk without a new commission |
| Archivist | Maintain immutable records, indexes, lineage and access logs | Flag incomplete records as administratively ineligible | Grant scientific or capital approval |
| Human owner / designated amendment approver | Set objectives and prospective governance policy | Halt work; reject amendments; grant or revoke authority | Convert failed or nonexistent tests into passed evidence |

## Binding authority

A domain pack must map every required authority to a named accountable actor and identify independent examiners. The same actor cannot propose, certify, and authorize the same candidate. Missing role coverage does not transfer power to the Conductor.

Independence for material decisions requires a separate brief, context, and reviewer. Separate process identities are configurable.

Discovery cannot certify itself. Experiment Conductor does not assemble Discovery assignments for the mission it is currently defending.

Record veto scope, reason, evidence, affected IDs, remediation, and issuer. A veto stays active until the issuing authority or its formally designated successor records clearance supported by new evidence. Routing and prioritization cannot suppress a veto. A board vote cannot override one.

Promotion requires all applicable gates and authorizations; risk permission alone is not scientific validation. Emergency stops take effect immediately and are archived afterward if waiting would increase exposure. Restart requires fresh clearance under the adopted policy.
