# Communication rules

The default is **1:1 Conductor routing**, with the Archivist and atomic registry as the system of record. Do not create a full-team room.

## Independent work

Give each specialist a narrow unknown, authorized input set, requested artifact, budget/deadline, and falsification objective. Preserve independent initial findings before sharing peer conclusions. Different model names are not proof of independent reasoning.

The Conductor may commission specific cross-agent work when dependencies justify it. Record participants, purpose, shared inputs, expected output and any loss of independence. A synthesis is a new artifact with parent links; it is not an independent replication.

## Handoffs

Use IDs and versions, not a chat transcript as the only record. Each handoff identifies sender, intended recipient/authority, trigger, question, inputs, output schema, constraints and return path. The Archivist retains original submissions, disagreements and subsequent revisions.

The Clock, Examiner, Prosecutor, Mechanic and Treasurer enter when their gate is triggered. A material integrity or risk alert may be routed immediately to its responsible authority; the Conductor cannot delay a protective stop.

## Limited board exception

When a crypto candidate reaches validation/promotion, a small Promotion Board may contain **Conductor + Examiner + Prosecutor + Mechanic + Treasurer**. It resolves recorded promotion, execution and capital-eligibility questions. It does not brainstorm alpha, rerun discovery by discussion, or vote away vetoes. Clock's data verdict remains a prerequisite.

Other domains declare equivalent participants in their adopted pack. Record the agenda, candidate versions, evidence, dissent, decisions and action owners; close the board when its purpose is complete.

No consensus-as-evidence. No hidden edits to another specialist's artifact. No promotion based only on a conversation.
