# Promotion and demotion protocol

Promote a versioned candidate only when its predeclared gates pass and its independent authorities permit the next stage. A status name is not proof that a gate ran.

## Reusable ladder

Hypothesis → research test/pass → validation/pass → sealed evaluation/pass → independent replication → forward shadow → simulated operation/paper → minimum bounded exposure → limited exposure → production.

These generic terms map the established crypto ladder to other domains. The domain pack must register its exact states, criteria and responsible actors before use. Replication and forward observation cannot be replaced with a vote.

## Crypto binding

**HYPOTHESIS → RESEARCH TEST → VALIDATION → HISTORICAL SEALED HOLDOUT → CROSS-VENUE REPLICATION → FORWARD SHADOW → PAPER → MICRO CAPITAL → LIMITED CAPITAL → PRODUCTION**

Cross-venue replication and a true future forward window refine the earlier ladder. Historical success alone cannot earn production capital. Provisional execution economics cannot advance a candidate beyond provisional validation.

## Transition record

Every transition records candidate/version, prior and requested states, gate IDs and results, applicable policy/version, data and code hashes, independent Examiner, Red Team disposition, active vetoes, relevant governor authorizations, and a Decision Record. Deterministic enforcement must reject missing, stale, mismatched or failed prerequisites.

A candidate cannot skip a required stage or reuse approvals issued for another version. Composite candidates require their own tests, including ablations.

## Freeze and contamination

Freeze rules, parameters, datasets, evaluation windows, costs, minimum samples, stresses and success/failure criteria before judgment. Seal holdout access. Any exposure of holdout outcomes is recorded. After a holdout-driven revision, create a new candidate version and obtain a new untouched future holdout; the old window cannot become pristine again.

## Demotion

The lab's established ladder allows **PRODUCTION → LIMITED → PAPER → SHADOW → RETIRED**. Severe integrity, execution or risk failures may halt operation immediately rather than waiting through each stage.

Domain-specific deterioration, exposure and restart thresholds must be frozen before operation. Missing thresholds block operation. Demotions, suspensions and retirements create Decision Records and appropriate Failure Records; retained historical success is not permission to restart.

“No action” / **NO TRADE** remains the default when eligibility, trustworthy data or risk clearance is absent.
