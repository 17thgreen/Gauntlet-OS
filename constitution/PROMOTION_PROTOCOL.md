# Promotion and demotion protocol

Promote a versioned candidate only when its predeclared gates pass and its independent authorities permit the **next specific authority**. A status name is not proof that a gate ran. Evidence KEEP on one uncertainty is not Operate-at-scale.

Promotion of **authority** requires evidence matched to that permission: demand, delivery quality, seller and buyer economics, workload actuals, operational reliability, and a revocation path.

## Reusable ladder

Hypothesis → research test → validation → sealed evaluation → independent replication → forward / shadow operation → bounded pilot → limited operation → production.

The domain pack registers exact states, criteria and actors before use. Replication and forward observation cannot be replaced with a vote.

Domain mappings live in the pack, not here. Historical success alone cannot earn production authority.

## Four fields on every transition

Record admission, execution state, evidence verdict (scope included), and the authority granted or refused. See [Experiment schema](../schemas/EXPERIMENT.md).

## Transition record

Every transition records candidate/version, prior and requested states, the four fields, gate IDs and results, applicable policy/version, data and code hashes, independent Examiner, Red Team disposition, active vetoes, relevant governor authorizations, and a Decision Record. Deterministic enforcement must reject missing, stale, mismatched or failed prerequisites.

A candidate cannot skip a required stage or reuse approvals issued for another version. Composite candidates require their own tests, including ablations.

## Freeze and contamination

Freeze rules, parameters, datasets, evaluation windows, costs, minimum samples, stresses and success/failure criteria before judgment. Seal holdout access. Any exposure of holdout outcomes is recorded. After a holdout-driven revision, create a new candidate version and obtain a new untouched future holdout; the old window cannot become pristine again.

## Demotion and return

Operate → restricted operation → Experiment → pause → retirement.

Severe integrity, execution or risk failures may halt operation immediately. Domain-specific deterioration, exposure and restart thresholds must be frozen before operation. Missing thresholds block operation. Demotions, suspensions and retirements create Decision Records and appropriate Failure Records; retained historical success is not permission to restart.

“No action” remains the default when eligibility, trustworthy data or risk clearance is absent. The domain pack names that state.
