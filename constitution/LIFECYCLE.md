# Lifecycle — Discover → Experiment → Operate

**Status:** RATIFIED 2026-09-16 (Governor)
**Governs:** every domain pack
**Does not:** set domain thresholds, spend ceilings, or specialist rosters

## Governing principle

Each stage earns a **specific next authority**. Every claim remains limited to the evidence that supports it. Every operating permission remains subject to measured performance.

Three distinctions are constitutional:

1. A promising proposal is not evidence that a business works.
2. A successful experiment is not sufficient evidence that an operation can scale.
3. An automation percentage is meaningless without an absolute limit on human workload.

## Stages

```
DISCOVER                         EXPERIMENT                        OPERATE
What deserves resources?         Does this proposition work?       Can we deliver it?
Opportunity + credible test      Executed test + disposition       Service under granted authority
```

Handoffs are documents. Discovery cannot stamp its own admission. Experiment cannot rewrite the mission to protect a favorite candidate. Operate cannot start because a demo is pretty.

### Discover

Find, challenge, and design something the fleet can substantially operate. Steal working machines; forbid copying them. Return the strongest opportunity **and** the smallest *credible* test of its critical assumptions — including a working product or service when demand cannot be tested without one.

Independence requires a **separate brief, context, and reviewer**. Separate Bot identities are a deployment choice, not proof of independence.

Discovery is scored on information gained, decision quality, cost, and downstream outcomes. Justified **INCONCLUSIVE** is a legal verdict. Easy kills are not a performance metric.

Reject a familiar idea because it fails *this* commission or adds no incremental value — not because it resembles existing work.

### Experiment

Execute an admitted commission. Measure. File artifacts. Dispose. Return a Finding if the test cannot answer the question.

Build the operating machinery the test needs (fulfillment, capture, reconciliation). That is Experiment work. **External commercial authority** is a separate field (see four-field status).

### Operate

Deliver under granted authority. Own fulfillment, acquisition/retention, money movement, incidents, suppliers, workload, and deterioration monitoring. Routine improvements stay here. A change of customer, value proposition, or major risk exposure requires a new commission.

Return path (not one-way): Operate → restricted operation → Experiment → pause → retirement.

## Four status fields (never collapse)

| Field | Establishes |
|-------|-------------|
| **Admission** | Is this commission specified and permitted? |
| **Execution** | Has work started, paused, or finished? |
| **Evidence** | What did the test establish, on what population? |
| **Authority** | What may the system do next? |

A test can finish with insufficient evidence. A mechanism can pass while the business fails economically. A limited pilot can exist without expansion rights.

## Observed vs estimated

Claimed **results** that have not been observed are dashes or UNTESTED. Explicitly labeled estimates, synthetics, and scenario models are permitted as `[E]` / `[H]` / `[A]` with assumptions stated. DEMO and synthetic rows cannot enter Examiner totals. Front-door metrics show observed outcomes only.

## Human workload (primary operating measures)

- Human minutes per accepted deliverable or transaction
- Fixed human hours per week
- Percentage of cases completed without human intervention
- Exception frequency and resolution time
- Quality and contribution margin at each volume step
- An **absolute human-hours cap** for the authority being granted

If an automation percentage is used, it is versus a documented manual baseline — never versus agent runtime. Workload gates the *authority being granted*, not every evidence KEEP.

## Promotion of authority

Evidence KEEP on one uncertainty does not grant Operate-at-scale. Authority promotion requires evidence matched to the permission: demand, delivery quality, economics (seller and buyer), workload actuals, operational reliability, and a revocation path.
